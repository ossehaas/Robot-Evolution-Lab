## Notes

- Every STL is already laid flat in its print orientation (the face that goes on the bed is at z = 0); all parts print without supports. Check the first layer of the large shells in your slicer.
- PLA: 0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers, 15 % gyroid. The part masses in the table assume exactly that.
- TPU 95A (Polymaker PolyFlex TPU95; ankle springs, sole discs, paws, ears, collar) prints in its own jobs through Elegoo's TPU adapter (it cannot share a job with the CANVAS PLA): 225 C nozzle, 35-45 C textured PEI, fan on, 20-30 mm/s (Polymaker: 210-230 C, bed 25-60 C, 30-50 mm/s), 0.2 mm layers, short slow retraction (~1 mm: long feed path), filament dried (70 C, 8 h).
- Ankle springs and sole discs are springs: print them SOLID (100 % infill, 4 walls) exactly as laid out (springs on their side, soles arms-down); their stiffness was computed for solid TPU. Paws, ears and collar: 3 walls, 15 % infill.
- Servo case screws: M2 self-tapping into the 1.6 mm case holes (the screws that come with the servo fit if they are >= 8 mm long). Horn screws: M3 into the aluminium horns' 2.5 mm holes (tap M3 if they are not threaded).
- Heat-set inserts go in after printing (soldering iron at ~220 C). Holes are 4.0 mm for M3 x 5 x 4.0 inserts.
- Knee pushrods: press a 683ZZ bearing (3 x 7 x 3) into each eye; crank and lever pins are M3 button head screws into short heat-set inserts. Set the servo zero so that crank and lever are parallel at the rest pose.
- `servo_map.json` lists the bus id, joint, direction sign and limits of every servo for the firmware.
